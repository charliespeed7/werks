# WERKS Brand Campaign - Design Brief

> For: Figma design system build
> Reference: Oliver Gareis typography principles
> Status: ready to execute once Figma MCP is connected

## The Campaign

**WORK. WORK. WORK. WORK. WERKS.**

The repetition uses the real word "work" four times. The fifth word shifts to the brand name "WERKS" as the payoff. The switch from work to werks IS the product. It handles the work.

Full bleed black and white photography. Bold neon type. Poster style. Then the URL.

## Visual Direction

### Photography
- Full bleed, edge to edge, no margins
- Black and white, high contrast, grain or halftone texture
- Subject: tradespeople mid-action. Not posed. Not smiling at camera. Real work, real grit. Subjects across the campaign:
  1. Gas engineer (hands on a boiler, pipes)
  2. Beautician (hands mid-treatment, not posed)
  3. Gardener (hands in soil, shears, hedging)
  4. Pool cleaner (water, nets, poolside)
  5. Tattooist (hands mid-ink, machine on skin)
- Diversity is in the image, not the type. Same campaign, different trades. Shows WERKS is a platform, not a niche product.
- Reference: editorial fashion photography energy applied to trades. Think Vogue meets utility. Rihanna "Work" video aesthetic but in a British trade context.

### Typography
- Neon font. Bold. Condensed or wide. The type IS the design.
- Treatment: the repeated "WERK" words stack or run across the image like a poster
- The final word "WERKS." lands differently. Different colour, different weight, or different size. It's the payoff.
- Type should feel like it's been printed on the image. Poster, not digital UI.
- No body copy. No subhead. No tagline. Just the word and the URL.

### Colour
- Black and white photo as the base
- Neon accent for the type. Electric green, hot pink, or electric blue. One colour only.
- The "WERKS." word could shift to a different neon colour or to white to land the payoff
- Dark, confident, not playful

### Layout
- Full bleed image
- Type overlaid on image, not in a separate band
- "WERK. WERK. WERK. WERK. WERKS." running across, stacked, or arranged as a poster composition
- **werks.ai** small, bottom corner, clean sans-serif. The only quiet element on the page.

### Scale
- Works as a billboard (seen from a distance, one word reads)
- Works as an Instagram ad (square or 9:16, type fills the frame)
- Works as a trade counter poster (A2 print, stuck on the wall at Wolseley)
- Works as a t-shirt (black shirt, neon print, just "WERKS." on the front)

## Execution Notes for Figma Build

### Components needed
1. **Poster frame** - full bleed image container with type overlay
2. **Neon type style** - bold, condensed, with glow effect (or simulated glow via layer styles)
3. **URL lockup** - small, clean, bottom-right placement
4. **Image treatment** - black and white with contrast and grain filter applied

### Variations to build
1. Billboard / wide format (16:9 or wider)
2. Instagram square (1:1)
3. Instagram story / vertical (9:16)
4. Trade counter poster (A2 portrait)
5. T-shirt design (black background, neon type only, "WERKS.")

### Typography specs (to be refined)
- Font options: a bold condensed grotesk (Anton, Druk, Oswald Bold) or a wide geometric (Arial Black, Gilroy Black)
- Neon treatment: bright fill colour with subtle outer glow
- The repetition should feel rhythmic, not random. Equal spacing or building intensity.

## The Feeling

This isn't an ad for software. This is a poster for a movement. The tradesperson sees it and thinks "finally, someone gets it." The repetition of WERK is the work itself. Repetitive, relentless, real. And WERKS is the thing that handles it.

## Don'ts

- No illustrations
- No stock photos of smiling plumbers
- No feature lists
- No "AI-powered" badges
- No gradients
- No icons
- No subhead explaining what WERKS does
- No "sign up" or "learn more" buttons
- No em dashes anywhere
