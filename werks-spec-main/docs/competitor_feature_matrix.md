## Executive Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **No competitor runs the full job lifecycle through AI.** Every AI answering service stops at lead capture or booking. Quoting, invoicing, payment chasing, and annual service loops are either absent or separate non-AI features in field service platforms. This is the WERKS lifecycle gap (steps 4–9) and it remains uncontested.

2. **The UK price anchor is £45/mo headline, but £65–100/mo in reality.** Down To Earth AI advertises £45/mo + £299 setup, but adding WhatsApp/SMS channels (£23/channel/mo) and overage (20p/min after 100 minutes) pushes the real cost to £65–100/mo. Clara AI (UK) is £69/mo flat. This gives WERKS more pricing room than the £45 headline suggests.

3. **The incumbent window for steps 1–3 is already closed.** Jobber shipped AI Receptionist in August 2025 (live for nearly a year). Housecall Pro shipped CSR AI. Commusoft has AI:den+ for emails. Simpro shipped 100+ AI features in July 2026 (RAIN/Cooper). WERKS cannot compete on "we have AI call answering and they don't" — the differentiator must be lifecycle depth + appliance memory + UK-specific trade focus.

4. **No competitor has appliance-level memory.** Jobber and Housecall Pro have customer/job history but nothing that records "Worcester Bosch Greenstar 30SI, serial X, installed Y, last serviced Z, next due W." This is the WERKS moat and it remains uncontested.

5. **UK-specific, trades-focused AI answering is a thin field.** Only Down To Earth AI, Callout AI, and Clara AI (UK) target UK trades specifically. The rest are US-focused or general SME. A UK-native, gas-engineer-specific AI partner with lifecycle depth is a genuinely open position.

6. **Feature gating is the hidden cost driver.** Most competitors gate key features (appointment booking, call transfers, CRM integrations) behind higher tiers. Rosie's $49 plan can't book appointments — you need $149/mo. Goodcall's unique-caller cap punishes marketing success. Smith.ai's per-call model gets expensive fast. WERKS's all-in-one approach avoids this nickel-and-diming.

7. **Multi-channel (voice + SMS + WhatsApp) is rare under $100/mo.** Trillet is the only US service bundling all three at $49/mo. Down To Earth AI charges £23/channel/mo extra. For UK gas engineers where WhatsApp is the primary customer communication channel, this is a significant gap.

## Section 5 — Competitive Positioning Summary

### Where WERKS Wins (Uncontested Space)

1. **Full lifecycle AI (steps 1–9):** No competitor runs the full job lifecycle through AI — capture → book → quote → invoice → chase payment → take payment → annual service loop. WERKS's differentiator is real and confirmed.

2. **Appliance-level memory:** No competitor has an appliance-level memory entity ("Worcester Bosch Greenstar 30SI, serial X, installed Y, last serviced Z, next due W"). Jobber/HCP have customer/job history but not appliance records. This is the WERKS moat.

3. **UK-native, gas-engineer-specific:** Only Down To Earth AI, Callout AI, and Clara AI target UK trades. None are gas-engineer-specific. None handle Gas Safe compliance, annual service loops, or boiler service records.

4. **WhatsApp at base price:** Only Trillet includes WhatsApp at base price ($49/mo). Down To Earth AI charges £23/mo extra. For UK gas engineers where WhatsApp is primary, this is a gap.

5. **No feature gating / nickel-and-diming:** Most competitors gate key features behind higher tiers (Rosie booking at $149, Goodcall caller caps, Smith.ai add-ons). WERKS's all-in-one approach avoids this.

### Where WERKS is Vulnerable

1. **Incumbent AI is already shipping:** Jobber (AI Receptionist since Aug 2025), Housecall Pro (CSR AI), Commusoft (AI:den+), Simpro (RAIN/Cooper July 2026). The "no incumbent has AI" window is closed for steps 1–3.

2. **Incumbent lifecycle depth:** Jobber and HCP already have quoting, invoicing, payments, and CRM as native features. If they add AI to those workflows, the lifecycle gap closes from the inside. This is an additive feature on data they already hold — estimated 12–18 month window.

3. **Price competition:** Answer.co.uk at £10/mo, Upfirst at $24.95/mo, Down To Earth AI at £45/mo. WERKS must justify premium pricing through lifecycle depth and appliance memory, not just call answering.

4. **Incumbent resources:** Jobber (250K+ users), HCP (200K+ pros), Simpro (enterprise). They have the data, engineering teams, and distribution to add AI features quickly.

### The Three Strategic Questions

**1. Where is the real price anchor?**
The UK headline anchor is £45/mo (Down To Earth AI) + £299 setup. But the real cost with channels (WhatsApp, SMS) and overage is £65–100/mo. Clara AI is £69/mo flat. WERKS has more pricing room than the £45 headline suggests.

**2. Does anyone run the whole line unattended?**
No. All stop at lead capture or booking. Quoting, invoicing, payment chasing, and annual service loops are absent or separate non-AI features. D3's differentiation is real and confirmed.

**3. How long is the incumbent window?**
Steps 1–3 (capture, qualify, book): **Already closed.** Jobber and HCP have shipped AI call answering.
Steps 4–8 (quote, invoice, chase, pay): **Open but narrowing** — 12–18 months. Incumbents already own these features as non-AI; adding AI is additive.
Appliance memory + annual service loop: **Fully open** — no competitor has this. This is WERKS's defensible long-term moat.
