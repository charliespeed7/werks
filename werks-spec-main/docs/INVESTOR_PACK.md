## 1. The Opportunity

2 billion people work in the informal economy. 60% of the world's workforce. They run trade businesses on phones, WhatsApp, paper diaries, and memory. Nobody serves them with software.

In the UK and US, trades businesses use spreadsheets or nothing. In Brazil and Africa, they run on WhatsApp and cash. The tools that exist — Jobber, Housecall Pro, Commusoft — are built for businesses that already think of themselves as businesses. They don't reach the plumber who runs everything from his phone and has never used software.

**WERKS is a phone-first, voice-first AI operating layer for small service businesses.** It lives where the owner lives — on their phone. It answers calls, captures leads, drafts quotes, sends invoices, chases payment, manages bookings, and remembers every customer and every appliance. The owner speaks in outcomes; WERKS maps them to agents.

**The moat is not the AI. It's the context layer** — memory, customer database, scheduling, and wallet. Agents commoditise over time. The context layer doesn't. When someone builds the best estimation agent on the planet, it still runs on our customer graph and pays through our wallet.

## 4. Competitive Landscape

### The field

12 AI answering services reviewed across UK and US. Key findings:

**Nobody runs the whole line unattended.** Every AI answering service stops at lead capture or booking. Quoting, invoicing, and payment chasing are either not offered or are separate non-AI features in field service platforms. The lifecycle gap (steps 4–9) is real and unoccupied.

**No competitor has appliance-level memory.** Jobber and Housecall Pro have customer/job history but nothing that records "Worcester Bosch Greenstar 30SI, serial X, installed Y, last serviced Z, next due W." This is the WERKS differentiator.

### Incumbent check

| Incumbent | AI status | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Jobber** | Shipped (AI Receptionist, Aug 2025) | 24/7 AI phone answering, lead capture, job booking. $29-99/mo + AI add-on |
| **Housecall Pro** | Shipped (CSR AI) | 24/7 AI call answering, chat, job booking. $59-79/mo + AI add-on |
| **Commusoft** | Shipped (AI:den+) | AI emails and scheduling outreach. Not voice yet |
| **Simpro** | Shipping (RAIN + Cooper AI, Jul 2026) | 100+ AI features across 3 brands |
| **Tradify** | Shipped (SmartTools) | AI-assisted writing, not call answering |

### The window

The incumbent window for steps 1–3 (call answering, lead capture, booking) is **closed** — Jobber and HCP have shipped. The remaining window is for steps 4–8 (AI-driven quoting, invoicing, payment chasing, appliance memory). That window is **12–18 months** before incumbents add AI to their existing workflows.

**WERKS's advantage:** the customer who isn't on Jobber or HCP yet, and the appliance-level memory neither of them has.

### UK price anchor

- Headline: £45/mo (Down To Earth AI) + £299 setup
- Real cost with channels and overage: £65–100/mo
- US equivalents: $49/mo (Trillet, Marlie)
- Premium UK: £149/mo (Callout AI)

WERKS prices above the answering-service anchor. The real floor is likely higher than the £45 headline suggests, giving WERKS more room than assumed.

**Recommendation:** Run Route 1 first (cheapest, fastest test), Route 2 in parallel (long lead time), Route 3 as research.

## 6. Product

### What WERKS does

1. Dedicated business phone number
2. AI call answering and lead capture
3. SMS and WhatsApp support
4. Calendar and booking management
5. Quote drafting and acceptance follow-up
6. Invoice drafting and payment chasing
7. Daily operational summary (The Daily Brief)
8. Customer and job memory (appliance-level)
9. Approval controls and audit trail

### Product principles

- Phone-first, voice-first where valuable
- WhatsApp-native
- Low setup effort, useful within first hour
- Calm and simple, proactive not noisy
- Transparent about what happened
- Safe by default, human approval where risk is material
- Every action auditable
- Revenue and time saved are more important than feature usage

### Architecture

WERKS is the orchestrator. It owns:
- **Memory** — what it knows about the business and its history
- **Customer database** — the relationship graph
- **Scheduling** — calendar and recurring cadence engine
- **Wallet** — payments in, payments out to third parties on the user's behalf

Agents (first-party and third-party) are interchangeable consumers of this context layer. Nothing bypasses the orchestrator.

### Pricing tiers

- **Free** — entry, limited out-of-the-box agents
- **Core (~$20/month)** — basic out-of-the-box agents (invoicing, inbox)
- **…up to ~£150/month** — full agent access

Open question: per-agent pricing ("hire and fire an agent") vs tier bundles. The "hire a digital employee" framing argues for pricing against the human they replace (an EA at £15k/year), not as cheap SaaS seats.
