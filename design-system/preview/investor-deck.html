01 — The problem

2 billion

People working in the informal economy · 60% of the world's workforce

Nobody serves them with software.

They run trade businesses on phones, messaging apps, paper diaries, and memory.

UK & US. Trades businesses use spreadsheets, paper, or nothing at all.

Brazil & Africa. Businesses run on messaging apps and cash.

The tools that exist — Jobber, Housecall Pro, Commusoft — are built for businesses that already think of themselves as businesses.

They never reach the plumber who runs everything from his phone and has never used software.

10 — Product principles

Calm, and accountable for every word that goes out.

A gas engineer, one hand, gloves on, in a driveway in June, on a four-year-old Android, with fifteen seconds and no signal.

  • —Phone-first. Voice is a first-class channel, not a demo.
  • —Messaging-agnostic. WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, SMS — we meet them there.
  • —Low setup effort. Useful within the first hour.
  • —Calm and simple. Proactive, never noisy.
  • —Transparent about what actually happened.
  • —Safe by default. Human approval where risk is material.
  • —Every action auditable.
  • —Revenue and time saved matter more than feature usage.
