The conversational layer

v1.0 designed the surfaces where a decision gets made. Most of what the owner actually sees is a conversation, and the card is one frame of it. This is the rest.

One

Werks does not get a bubble. It speaks from a rail, because it is not a participant.

Two

A draft is an unsent bubble, sitting where the sent message will sit.

Three

The Dot is the only thing that is alive. Five states, one shape.

Four

Everything spoken is also on screen, always, in full.

What v1.1 amends in v1.0

voice-first.md rule 8

"Never speak back" is now scoped to customer-facing voice. Werks may speak to the owner, under six conditions, and never as the only channel. Customer-facing voice is still Horizon 2.

05-motion.md

"The Dot sits still" becomes "the Dot sits still unless Werks is working". It breathes while thinking, and stops the instant the work does. It never breathes to attract attention.

The four voices

Exactly four things can appear in a thread. Who is speaking is carried by position and surface, never by colour, so it survives sunlight, 200% text and colour blindness.

Voice states

Nine states. Six are from v1.0. Ambient, armed and speaking are new, and speaking can happen from any of them.

Off

no dot

Werks is not running. Absence is the state.

Ambient

On. Nothing being recorded, no wake word armed.

Thinking

Working on something now, and you are waiting for it. 3.2s breath.

Working, background

A ring. Out on an errand for days. It never moves.

Listening

Real microphone amplitude, never a canned loop.

Live transcript

The one place words arrive one at a time. This is the owner's own speech coming back, and the reveal is proof the microphone heard those words. Drafts never stream: approval requires reading a finished sentence.

The uncertain span

Boiler at 14 Elm Road, replaced the diverter valve. Postcode . Serial 7731 600 123.

Postcodes, serials, phone numbers and money are always marked the first time they appear, whatever the recogniser's own confidence says. No percentages: uncertainty is stated, never scored.

Werks speaking

The full text is on screen before the first word is spoken. The word being read is primary, the text ahead is secondary. A single colour change, no highlight box, no karaoke fill. Stop and Mute are always in the same place, always 48px.

Voice and visual together

Never speak an option that is not on screen. Never more than three. The spoken words and the on-screen words are identical, so saying one back is unambiguous.

Handoff

FromBehaviour
Voice → touchSpeech stops in 120ms. Everything stays. No "are you sure you want to switch".
Touch → voiceThe utterance applies to the current context. "Make it Friday" edits the open draft.
Voice → keyboardTranscript carried in, cursor at the end. Nothing re-dictated.
Keyboard → voiceDictated words append at the cursor. Typed words are never replaced.

The transcript is the state. Changing input never resets it, and there is no such thing as voice mode.

Error recovery

What happened, what it means, what to do now. And in every case the keyboard is one tap away, and the audio is never thrown away.

FailureCopyRecovery
Too quietCan't hear you. Move somewhere quieter or type it.Said at second two, not second thirty
Background noiseToo much noise here. Try again or type it.Keyboard, and retry
Cut off mid-sentenceThat got cut off. Here's what was caught.Carry on dictating into the same field
Misheard postcodeMarked as an uncertain spanTap for alternates, or type it
Misheard serialRead back in groups: 7731 600 123Tap to fix, or scan the plate
Wrong customer matchedWerks matched this to Sarah Whitfield. Two customers are on this number.Change customer
OfflineNo signal. Saved on this phone, it'll go when you're back.Queued. Audio always kept.

Do

Second failure in a row: open the keyboard automatically with the partial transcript in it. Werks does not ask a third time.

Don't

Silently correct a misheard postcode to one the system prefers. It marks the span instead.

Motion

A conversation has a sequence, and the sequence is information. That is the only reason anything moves here. No transition exceeds 280ms; the two long values are loop periods, not transitions.

TokenValueUse
--w-duration-word90msOne word landing in a live transcript
--w-duration-bubble200msA message arriving. Fade plus 8px rise, always upward.
--w-duration-turn240msThe thread settling after a turn
--w-duration-collapse240msA quiet block opening
--w-duration-bargein120msSpeech stopping. A ceiling, not a target.
--w-duration-breath3200msOne breath of the Dot. A resting adult breathes about every 4 seconds.
--w-stagger-word40msBetween words in a transcript
--w-stagger-bubble60msBetween messages arriving together. Five maximum.
--w-ease-breathcubic-bezier(0.37, 0, 0.63, 1)Symmetrical sine. A breath has no accent; a pulse does.
--w-ease-arrivecubic-bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)Things arriving, decelerating into place

What does not move

Never

Drafts streaming in word by word. They appear complete: approval requires reading a finished sentence.

Never

A typing indicator for Werks. Three animated dots is a person pretending to type.

Never

Anything in an escalation. Movement reads as an alarm, and an alarm is something you turn off.

Never

Sound. No earcons, no chimes, no send whoosh. The only audio is Werks's own speech.

Every state in this layer has a word, a timer, a colour or a shape carrying the same fact, which is what makes it safe to switch all of it off. Turn on prefers-reduced-motion and nothing here becomes unknowable.
