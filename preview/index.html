Trustworthy, calm, simple, proactive. Not noisy. The campaign shouts on a billboard so the product never has to shout on a phone.

The Dot

The full stop, reused as the product's only live indicator. It means one thing: Werks is on it. It appears in five places and nowhere else. It never pulses.

6px inline 8px row 12px voice Never a count. Never a red badge. Never pulsing.

Colour

Black and paper, one signal colour, four status colours used sparingly. Primary actions are black, not coloured: it reads in sunlight, it looks like print, and it leaves colour free to mean something.

The conversational layer

v1.1. Bubbles, threads, quiet blocks, the full voice flow, and the Dot as a living thing. Most of what the owner actually sees is a conversation, and the approval card is one frame of it.

Conversation

Message bubbles, threads, inline actions, quiet blocks. Werks gets no bubble: it speaks from a rail.

Voice

Nine states, ambient listening, live transcript, interruption, error recovery, voice plus visual.

Patterns

The Brief as a conversation, approval aloud, nudges, escalation, multi-turn booking, handoff.

Alive

Off, ambient, thinking, working, listening. One shape, five states, and absence is one of them.
